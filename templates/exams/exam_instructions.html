1 General Instructions

  • Please read all instructions carefully before starting the examination.
  • The countdown timer starts as soon as you click Start Exam.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection throughout the test.
  • Your selected answers are automatically saved in real-time as you navigate.
  • If you close or refresh the page, your progress and time remaining will be preserved when you resume.

4 Exam Controls & Navigation

  • Question Palette: Click any cell in the right sidebar matrix to jump directly to that question.
  • Save & Next: Saves your response and advances to the next question.
  • Previous: Navigates back to the preceding question.
  • Clear Response: Deselects your choice for the current question.
  • Mark for Review: Flags the question for later review.

5 Autosave, Timer & Submission

The exam timer is server-authoritative. Once time expires, your exam will automatically submit. Before final submission, a pre-submission modal summary displays your total answered, unanswered, and review counts.
